InkHanzi — Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Last updated: 2026-10-27

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Your email address (only if you create an account). We do not require a real name, phone number, or date of birth.
  • Learning data: Your learning progress, quiz results, and streak records (stored locally on your device; synced to cloud only if you are logged in).
  • Purchase records: Subscription status and transaction identifiers (processed by Google Play and RevenueCat; we do not store your payment details).
  • Device information: Anonymous device identifier used for fraud prevention by our payment provider.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide login functionality and synchronize your learning progress across devices.
  • To verify your subscription status and manage your premium access.
  • To send you service notices (e.g., cloud service termination notice as described in our Terms of Service).

We do NOT use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase (authentication and data storage): Processes your email address and login credentials. Supabase is GDPR compliant and hosts data in the region you select.
  • RevenueCat (subscription management): Processes purchase verification and subscription status. RevenueCat does not store your payment card details.
  • Google Play Billing: Handles all payment transactions. Google's privacy policy applies to payment data.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your learning data is stored primarily on your local device. Cloud synchronization is optional and encrypted in transit.

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS) for all network communications.

Access to our backend databases is restricted by Row Level Security policies.

5. Data Retention

  • Local data: Retained on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data.
  • Cloud data: Retained while your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated cloud data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

6. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Export your data
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Delete your account and data
  • Withdraw consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at goblincraft.dev@gmail.com or use the in-app account deletion feature.

We respond to all data subject requests within 30 days as required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

InkHanzi is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Material changes will require you to re-consent within the app.

The current version is always available at this URL and within the app.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact: goblincraft.dev@gmail.com
