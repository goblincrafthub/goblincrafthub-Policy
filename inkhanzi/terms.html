1. Service Description

InkHanzi is a Chinese language learning application designed to help users learn Chinese characters, words, pronunciation, reading, and cultural knowledge. The application is designed with an offline-first architecture: all course content, audio files, and learning materials are bundled locally on your device.

Cloud services (login, progress sync, subscription verification) are optional enhancements. The core learning experience remains fully functional without an internet connection.
